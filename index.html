Slate move OPTIONAL · OFF

Shoot one static full-body slate. This punches in from head-and-shoulders to full body, holds, then settles closer — so you get both frames without the floor space for a wide shot.

Every pixel comes from your frame. Nothing is generated, nothing is enlarged. If the move would need to enlarge the picture it is refused rather than softened.

Casting offices disagree on this. Some coaches teach it; at least one casting director asks for no pan at all. The hold at full body is there for that reason. Check the instructions on your breakdown.
Focus and sharpness. Cropping cannot change focus — whatever the lens did during the slate is baked in, and the punch-in magnifies it. Lock focus and exposure before you shoot the slate.

A tighter crop is also fewer pixels. From a 1080p slate the closest frame is a 2.4× upscale and reads soft, so the app refuses rather than shipping a mushy tape. Shoot the slate at the largest size your phone offers.
