How this works

Five steps, left to right. You can skip any of them — nothing here blocks the record button.

01 · PREP

Load your sides. PDF works, including the ones Actors Access sends with the watermark and the instructions page — it strips all that out. Or paste the text.

It finds the characters and picks yours from the filename. Tap the × on any character who is not in your scene.

Then mark the scene. Tap ▶ on the line where it starts and ■ where it ends — casting usually writes those by hand, so they are not in the file. Everything outside dims but stays readable.

Reader booth. Once the scene is locked, record every line that is not yours, in your own voice. Record a line as many times as you like and keep the one you want. There is no synthetic voice in this app, on purpose — a robot reader gives you nothing to play against.

02 · SHOOT

Turn the camera on and look at yourself before you commit. Check the light and the background, set the format — casting is 16:9 landscape, social is 9:16 vertical — and the frame changes shape to match.

Then pick how you are working:

  • Solo, or with a reader. Nothing on screen but the frame.
  • Scene partner. Plays back the lines you recorded and hands you the floor. Your lines are never shown.
  • Teleprompter. Your lines on screen, at whatever level you want to see them.

Press record and you get 3 · 2 · 1 to get set. Nothing is captured during the count. Tap to cancel.

Got a Bluetooth clicker? Turn on REMOTE and you never touch the phone.

On the stage, LISTEN FOR MY CUE makes the scene partner wait until you have actually finished your line before it plays the next one.

03 · CUT

Pick a take, set the handles, choose a size. It really re-encodes — this is the step that replaces a separate compressor app. It runs in real time, so a ninety second take takes about ninety seconds.

04 · SAVE

When you keep a take it goes to your phone straight away, into your camera roll with everything else you shoot. Browser storage is a cache and it can be cleared without warning — a take that only lives in here is not saved. Anything not yet on your phone is flagged.

05 · SEND

Nothing gets uploaded from here. This is a list of your files, named the way casting expects — NAME_Role_Tk1.mp4 — sitting on your phone. Open Actors Access, pick them, upload, press send.

What it will not do

It does not measure your framing. Without a face detector, headroom and eyeline are unknown, and it says so rather than guessing.

It does not judge your acting. Selection and sequencing only.

It does not use an AI voice for your reader.

It does not upload for you.

Where things live

Everything is on this device. No account, no server, nothing sent anywhere. Clearing your browser data clears the app — which is the whole reason takes go to your phone the moment you keep them.

Everything here is optional. PREP supports the work; it does not gate RECORD.

Slate move OPTIONAL · OFF

Shoot one static full-body slate. This punches in from head-and-shoulders to full body, holds, then settles closer — so you get both frames without the floor space for a wide shot.

Every pixel comes from your frame. Nothing is generated, nothing is enlarged. If the move would need to enlarge the picture it is refused rather than softened.

Casting offices disagree on this. Some coaches teach it; at least one casting director asks for no pan at all. The hold at full body is there for that reason. Check the instructions on your breakdown.
Focus and sharpness. Cropping cannot change focus — whatever the lens did during the slate is baked in, and the punch-in magnifies it. Lock focus and exposure before you shoot the slate.

A tighter crop is also fewer pixels. From a 1080p slate the closest frame is a 2.4× upscale and reads soft, so the app refuses rather than shipping a mushy tape. Shoot the slate at the largest size your phone offers.
