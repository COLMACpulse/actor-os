Web capability probe

ACTOR OS · what this browser actually gives us

This page has to be served over https. Opening it from a downloaded file blocks the camera and the microphone, and every test below will fail for the wrong reason. Push it to GitHub Pages and open that address on the phone.
1 · This device—
Browser, whether it is running as an installed app, and which APIs are present. Nothing is switched on yet.



2 · Rear camera—



Opens the back camera and reads back what it will let us control —
zoom, focus, exposure, torch. This is the answer to whether the app can ever behave
like the camera app.



3 · Front camera—



Same reading for the selfie camera, which is the one you shoot on.





4 · How big a picture—



Asks for 4K, then for 1080p at 60. Reports what came back rather than
what was asked for.



5 · Recording format—



Records four seconds and reports the file it actually produced — type,
size, bitrate. Not what the browser claims it supports.



6 · A real take—



This is the one that decides the product. Camera, microphone,
recorder and speech recognition all running at once for thirty seconds, with a
sound played through the speaker partway in — the same load as scene partner.
Talk the whole time; read anything. It reports whether the footage survived, whether
the recogniser heard you, and whether playing the reader broke either.





7 · Keeping files—



How much room there is, and whether the phone will promise not to
delete takes when it needs space.
